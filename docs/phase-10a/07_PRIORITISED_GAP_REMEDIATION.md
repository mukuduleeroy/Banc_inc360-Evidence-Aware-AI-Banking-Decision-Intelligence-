## Business evidence and source authority

Measured retention/revenue/product uptake, validated commercial value and independent supplier/FX authenticity remain UNVERIFIED. A future authorized pilot can define outcome measures and baselines; do not invent uplift estimates now. Similarly confirm official organizer wording/eligibility directly before claiming external certification: this audit used the seven requirements/weights supplied in the task, not an authenticated organizer document.

## Recommended smallest sequence

1. Before the pitch, correct **spoken/demo claims**, not historical accepted reports: make the proxy, historical-ML, cross-sell and companion boundaries explicit; prepare existing artifact navigation. This needs no new application development.
2. Resolve the explicit upsell requirement with organizers/product expertise. If it is required, do not claim full coverage without a genuine implemented/reproducible upgrade relationship; request a tightly scoped approved corrective phase.
3. Agree the high-value definition and whether the existing activity proxy is sufficient. If not, design the smallest evidence-supported additive classification rather than replacing accepted analytics or fabricating profit.
4. Treat live churn inference, broad product catalogues, profit models and real-world uplift as separately specified/approved work; none is a quick safe last-minute add-on.

Do not spend the final preparation window on more visual polish, arbitrary extra AI models, relaxed gates, cosmetic upsell labels or automatic dataset switching merely to inflate feature count. Preserve the verified build and focus on direct business coverage.
