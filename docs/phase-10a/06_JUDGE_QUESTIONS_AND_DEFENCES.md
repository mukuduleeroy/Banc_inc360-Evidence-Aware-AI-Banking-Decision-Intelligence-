## Defensive phrasing to retain

- “Higher observed activity,” not “our most profitable customers.”
- “Observed next-90-day inactivity target,” not “verified churn.”
- “Eligible for staff review,” not “bank-approved offer.”
- “One implemented Savings cross-sell rule; no upsell capability yet.”
- “Verified historical experiment; no operational fitted inference.”
- “Companion-only snapshot activation; original dashboards remain on baseline.”
- “No measured retention, product-uptake or revenue uplift.”

The six-gate engine can be a compelling governance differentiator without making unsupported business claims. Honest boundaries do not substitute for closing a genuinely missing upsell requirement.
