# 36 hard business/banking judge questions

Use concise answers first; open the named evidence only if asked. All banking outcomes remain prototype scope. This is a prepared defence, not a claim that the team has received organizer/bank approval or passed a fresh Phase 11 test run.

| # | Challenge | Answer and evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | What specific banking problem are you solving? | The gap between observing customer behavior and justifying a relationship-manager action. We combine historical activity, product ownership, eligibility, explicit reasons and local human review (`ui_views`, `engine.evaluate`, `ReviewService`), rather than claiming an automated retention campaign. |
| 2 | How is this more than a standard BI dashboard? | A proposed Savings discussion passes six explicit permission gates and produces a Passport; blocked cases retain reasons, replay shows why a decision changes, and local review binds evidence/version. Charts are the entry point, not the authority. This is governance differentiation, not a claim of a unique proprietary ML algorithm. |
| 3 | Why are 47,928 customers ABSTAIN? | At least one blocking gate withholds this sales action. Among ABSTAIN cases, 39,928 have current restrictions, 27,483 lack rationale, 17,391 insufficient history and 10,000 already hold Savings; codes overlap. HISTORY_BORDERLINE also appears on 1,134 cases with another blocker. See Executive reason chart/`analytics.reason_distribution`. |
| 4 | Doesn't 95.856% abstention make the system ineffective? | It makes coverage/constraints visible instead of manufacturing permissions. It may surface a narrow prototype policy or data/business constraints that the bank should inspect. We do not equate withholding with success, nor relax eligibility to improve a metric. Commercial utility needs a measured pilot. |
| 5 | Why only 2,052 supported cases? | These meet the present integrity/history/method/status/product/human-control checks for a Savings discussion. There are 40,000 recorded gaps and 12,517 gaps with rationale, but those are not eligible-offer counts. No policy tuning was done to increase SUPPORTED. |
| 6 | Are those 2,052 your high-value customers? | No. SUPPORTED is permission for staff review. Only 23 are in the global Higher activity band. Original queue priority is triage, not economic value (`engine.evaluate`, source-derived aggregate in Phase 10A). |
| 7 | Does transaction volume equal customer value or profit? | No. It is gross absolute qualifying 180-day USD turnover. The explicit label is High Relationship Activity—Potential Value Review; 10,003 meet the unchanged >=.8 percentile boundary. No profit, margin, balance, LTV or future revenue is calculated. |
| 8 | Do you treat Retail and Corporate customers identically? | Source segments can be filtered, but the accepted percentile remains global. We do not claim a common commercial threshold is economically calibrated across Retail/SME/Corporate/NGO/Trust. Segment assignment/effective dates are source-provided and unknown, not inferred clustering. |
| 9 | Can you predict actual customer churn today? | No live fitted customer model exists. Operational output is observed recency and deterministic review triage. Historical ML tests future 90-day recorded inactivity, not confirmed relationship termination. Phase 10C sets conditions for any future isolated research ranking. |
| 10 | What would a manager do with an inactivity signal? | Inspect history, current eligibility and the Passport; use the existing limited review/defer/reject workflow. No dedicated automatic re-engagement treatment or full investigation assignment system exists for every ABSTAIN case. Appropriate real contact still needs bank approval and evidence. |
| 11 | Prove this is cross-selling, not recommending an existing product. | Known holdings are parsed, Savings absence established and repeated deposit rationale inspected. All supported canonical cases have 2–3 known existing products and no Savings. G5 suppresses already-held Savings; the selected panel displays actual G4/G5 (`render_savings_evidence`). |
| 12 | Your holdings catalogue has ten labels; do you recommend ten products? | No. Those labels describe existing ownership. The only operational prototype recommendation is a Savings discussion. Other product proposals are unsupported by current G5. No rates, lending offers or inferred preferences are supplied. |
| 13 | Is synthetic upselling a genuine banking recommendation? | It is a genuine hypothetical *upgrade relationship*: known synthetic Standard tier 1 → Premium tier 2 in one family, with eligibility/need checks. It is not an actual BancABC product or real-customer recommendation. No source evidence supports real tiers; the limitation is visible. |
| 14 | Why separate DEMO_* statuses? | To prevent illustrative fixtures from becoming SUPPORTED or entering original totals/queue/history. Seven independent cases yield 1 eligible, 3 missing, 3 not eligible. Their source ownership and hypothetical policy are never attached to real aliases (`phase10b_upsell`). |
| 15 | What prevents unsuitable recommendations? | Integrity/history checks, known ownership gap/rationale, Active/Verified current checks, approved-method restriction and mandatory human review. ABSTAIN dominates CAUTION. Unknown evidence withholds permission; a large activity or model score cannot override G4/G5/G6. These remain prototype controls, not bank suitability certification. |
| 16 | What does “Approve” really do? | It records APPROVED_LOCAL against current SUPPORTED evidence with reason, explicit reviewer and acknowledgment. It sends no message, changes no account and approves no financial product. CAUTION/ABSTAIN cannot receive local approval (`record_review`). |
| 17 | Who authenticates the reviewer? | Nobody in this prototype: DEMO-RM-01 and other labels are explicit unverified attribution, not staff login, role authority or identity verification. Production authentication/access governance would be separate required work. |
| 18 | Is your audit trail permanent and tamper-proof? | Local SQLite events/snapshots survive restart on retained storage and application triggers/revisions protect normal writes. The database owner remains trusted; it is unencrypted and not tamper-proof. Cloud local storage can be ephemeral. Prior actual restart evidence exists. |
| 19 | Can you detect stale evidence? | Changed dataset/manifest/policy/decision fingerprints or revisions block stale review commits and require refresh/inspection. This is content freshness, not proof that undated KYC snapshots remain current in calendar time. Staff verification is still required. |
| 20 | How are customer identities protected? | The presentation uses stable keyed aliases, no raw IDs/emails/names in analytic tables; free-text evidence is constrained/sanitized. A pseudonym is not complete anonymization or public release permission. Keys, source/derived rows, databases and customer screenshots remain private. |
| 21 | Did you copy real customers into synthetic demonstrations? | No. Phase 9's 14-row CSV generator and Phase 10B's DEMO-UP fixtures are independently invented code fixtures. Synthetic product tiers are not lightly altered bank records or joined to real customers. Examples and provenance are inspectable. |
| 22 | Can a bank upload any dataset? | No. Phase 9 accepts a defined compatible UTF-8 transaction CSV, 5 MiB/25,000 rows, already-normalized USD values, known schema/types/linkage and explicit cutoff. Unsupported inputs fail closed; no general mapping or FX conversion is promised. |
| 23 | Does activation update the nine original dashboards? | No. It changes the companion registry/preview only. The main app retains its configured accepted source; explicit rollback returns companion selection without overwriting canonical files or old review state. This isolation is deliberate. |
| 24 | What happens with duplicates or missing evidence? | Repeated customer/account rows are normal; exact transaction duplicates are reported/deduplicated by existing builder rules, conflicting IDs/ownership rejected. Blank eligibility/ownership is not invented as Verified or empty holdings. Invalid ingestion cannot activate; engine evidence failures withhold actions. |
| 25 | Is the data completely leakage-free? | We found explicit pre-cutoff features, separate labels, training-only scaling, no current KYC/holdings/ID inputs and controlled tests. Feed completeness/availability, timezone, current snapshot dates and repeated-customer/generalization limits remain. We do not certify absence of every leakage risk. |
| 26 | Where is the AI, and is .965 accuracy? | The actual historical evaluator fitted logistic/HGB against recency on five features; two recorded runs match. .965207 is logistic ROC-AUC, not accuracy; AP=.982397. Calibration/live inference is absent; no retention improvement follows from those metrics. |
| 27 | Could a model quietly change your queue priority? | It must not. Original priority is .45 capped recency + .35 global activity percentile + .20 service issue. No experimental model score enters Case or original priority. G3 blocks unapproved model-driven action; separate research cannot be disguised as a rule flag. |
| 28 | Why should BancABC trust this tomorrow? | It should trust an inspectable prototype enough to evaluate it, not use it blindly for banking actions. Local source/decision hashes, reasons, tests and human controls support review; product/model/data/security validation and bank authorization remain necessary before production. |
| 29 | How scalable is it? | One supplied million-row transaction extract and 50,000-customer index are evidenced; chunked build/caching/pagination improve local use. There is no production concurrency/latency SLA, multi-bank integration or proof at tens of millions of customers. Health-response timings are not full dashboard-load benchmarks. |
| 30 | How would you integrate it into banking systems? | Only through separately approved data contracts, access controls, product policies, model governance, integration and an audited pilot. Today there are no live banking APIs or customer communications. Preserve gate authority and source lineage rather than connecting a send button first. |
| 31 | What is innovative here? | The inspectable path from evidence to gated permission, explained abstention, what-if replay and accountable human review, with synthetic/research boundaries explicit. We do not claim novel estimators, external originality validation, competitor superiority or guaranteed judging points. |
| 32 | How would you measure actual business impact? | An authorized pilot with a predefined eligible population, comparison design and outcome definitions: incremental product uptake/retention, unsuitable contact rate, staff review effort and customer outcomes. No such measured pilot or causal uplift exists today; do not invent a forecast. |
| 33 | What financial return can you promise? | None from this evidence. Neither 2,052 reviewable cases nor top-500 inactivity precision is revenue. Fees, margin, costs, demand, consent and actual response would be needed for a credible return estimate. We present business potential as a hypothesis to test. |
| 34 | Did 647 tests pass in this release phase, and is the freeze perfectly clean? | No new full run was performed. The exact prior 647-pass receipt and 96-source manifest verify. One protected macOS folder-metadata file differs from its old checksum; banking/source/state checks match. We disclose it and retain a CONDITIONAL freeze, without deleting caches or rewriting a baseline. |
| 35 | Are the judging weights, deadline and submission format official? | The requirements/20–30–30–10–10 weights are user-supplied through Phase 10A. No authenticated organizer brief, portal, deadline or format was found locally. The dated team DOCX is not organizer confirmation; these must be verified before actual submission. |
| 36 | What would you build next—and why not now? | Approved real product-upgrade evidence, a bank-agreed value/churn definition, source/coverage validation and a measured pilot come before operational expansion. Phase 10C allows only conditional future research; no new predictive service is implemented or trained in this freeze. Final preparation focuses on truthful evidence and reproducible delivery. |
