## Media/publication acceptance checklist

- Obtain permission for dataset-derived aggregate/profile exposure and required brand/team presentation identity.
- No keys, secrets, raw IDs, student numbers, emails, phones, account/transaction IDs, confidential file paths or unsanitized notes on screen.
- Real aliases remain pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous; avoid presenting individual transaction amounts publicly without permission.
- Label independent synthetic fixtures, historical/recorded results, simulated replay and local review correctly.
- Do not infer a sanctioned recording/upload platform, final format or external submission portal; verify first.
- Keep original private evidence untouched. No edited/sanitized copy or video is claimed to exist until actually created and reviewed.
