# Final feasibility decision — CONDITIONAL GO

**CONDITIONAL GO for a future isolated, non-operational predictive-inactivity research capability.** Architecture and five-feature compatibility are feasible; saved model state, target/coverage assurance, validation against substantial shift and reproducible inference are not complete. This is **not implementation authorization** and not a claim that inference is runnable now. **NO-GO for immediate calibrated churn probabilities or any operational banking action.**

## Answers required for the final decision

- **Can a fitted model be reused? No saved fitted model/scaler is available.** Code recipes/feature definitions/experiment metadata can be reused, not learned state. A separately supplied artifact would need independent verification.
- **Is additional training needed? Yes, if a fitted inference capability is pursued from current artifacts.** It must be newly and explicitly authorized offline fitting that captures the full preprocessing/estimator bundle in isolated storage. Current CLI repetition alone writes metrics, not models. No fitting occurred here.
- **Are calibrated probabilities defensible now? No.** High AUC/AP/top-500 precision do not establish probability calibration, and prevalence shift alone does not quantify its error.
- **Is customer-level ranking technically feasible? Yes, conditionally.** The five inputs exist; strict eligibility/admission and exact fitted preprocessing can support research-only relative inactivity ranking after artifact and validation requirements. No scores were produced in this audit.
- **Can it remain isolated? Yes by design.** A standalone read-only research companion need not touch original Case/gates/priority, dashboards, review database or either existing companion's state. Isolation must be tested before delivery.
- **Is it worth implementing before the hackathon? Not as an immediate/rushed addition on current evidence.** Keep Option A; pursue Option B only with explicit permission, adequate validation/rehearsal time and the conditions below. More modelling does not automatically improve the submission.

## Conditions before any new model work

1. **Explicit new authorization** for fitting/artifact creation and any implementation. Phase 10C is read-only and provides none.
2. **Agreed target/use:** future recorded non-fee inactivity, not confirmed churn; defined cohort, horizon, closure/censoring treatment and limits on bank interpretation.
3. **Data evidence and permissions:** source/units/point-in-time availability, mature follow-up/completeness and extraction/generation assumptions reviewed; authorized local data/synthetic scenario and audience; no public model/data release assumed.
4. **Locked protocol:** exact ordered five-feature schema/precision, preprocessing recipe, chronology/label maturity, model-selection and validation plan, calibration assessment and predeclared research acceptance criteria. No threshold or minimum metric is invented in this audit.
5. **Protection/isolation design:** preserve the 647-case baseline, all original source/state hashes and sixteen columns; new research namespace only, no fitted action/G3 override or synthetic-bank fact mixing.

## Conditions before a scoring interface or demonstration

6. Obtain the **complete fitted trusted bundle** through the separately approved process; preserve log1p exactly once, training-only scaler/model and all hashes/versions. Prove parity and deterministic load/score behavior; never fit in UI.
7. Evaluate target/selection/shift across additional time windows and intended domains, with repeated/unseen-customer limits explicit. The already inspected July holdout cannot be recycled as a newly blind selection test. If completeness or target meaning remains unsupported, reduce the claim to extract-specific/synthetic engineering research or retain Option A; do not imply live banking validity.
8. Assess probability reliability and state the result honestly. **Rank-only research may disclose uncalibrated scores; calibrated percentages require separately successful calibration and independent reliability validation.** Do not create arbitrary risk bands or per-customer confidence intervals.
9. Validate input domains, unsupported/missing/insufficient-history cases and denominator reconciliation; never fill missing evidence or silently score all 50,000. Changed dataset/source semantics require validation, not automatic Phase 9 activation-to-scoring.
10. Execute all 647 prior cases plus new feature-order/preprocessing/artifact-tamper/no-fit/missing-domain/namespace/privacy/state-isolation tests; verify exact original decisions/priorities/reasons/aliases and hashes. Rehearse safely with a truthful frozen fallback and no customer data publication.

Operational use, calibrated churn claims, outreach or product/financial authorization would require separate business/model-risk evidence and governance beyond this proposal. Current G3/G4/G5/G6 cannot be weakened to enable them. No implementation, artifact, threshold, fitted score or permission was created in this phase.
